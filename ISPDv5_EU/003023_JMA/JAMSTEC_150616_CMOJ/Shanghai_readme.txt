Readme for Shanghai station data
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data source 
Monthly Report of the Central Meteorological Observatory Tokyo Japan 

Datasets
  Shanghai 3 time a day sea level pressure data 1920-1941
 
Station information
  location: 31deg 15'N 121deg 30'E (1920-1940)
            31deg 17'N 121deg 28'E (1941)
  altitude: 2.4m (1920-1933)
            11.7m (1934-1940)
            3.3m (1941)
            2.8m (1944)
  time: local time (+8 UTC) 1920-1938 6
                   (+9 UTC) 1938 7-1941
  unit: +700 mmHg
  altitude adjustment  data are station pressure 
                       for sea level adjustment see CMOJ_sl_SHANGHAI.xls
  gravity adjustment -0.9 mmHg

 

Data files
  SHANGHAI_yyyy.xls
    yyyy: year
  CMOJ_sl_SHANGHAI.xls


Data format
  Month day (local time) 6 14 22
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31



Data Policy
   This dataset is available for research only and not allowed to provide it to third party.
