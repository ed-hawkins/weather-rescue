Readme for Tsingtau station data
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data source 
Monthly Report of the Central Meteorological Observatory Tokyo Japan 

Datasets
  Tsingtau 3 to 6 time a day sea level pressure data 1918-1941
 
Station information
  location: 36deg 04'N 120deg 19'E (1918-1941)
  altitude: 78.6m (1918-1941)
  time: local time (+8 UTC) 1918-1938 6
                   (+9 UTC) 1938 7-1941
  unit: +700 mmHg
  altitude adjustment  data are station pressure 
                       for sea level adjustment see CMOJ_sl_TSINGTAU.xls
  gravity adjustment -0.6 mmHg

 

Data files
  TSINGTAU_yyyy.xls
    yyyy: year
  CMOJ_sl_TSINGTAU.xls


Data format
1918,1924-1929
  Month day (local time) 2 6 10 14 18 22
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31


1919-1923,1930-1941
  Month day (local time) 6 14 22
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31





Data Policy
   This dataset is available for research only and not allowed to provide it to third party.
