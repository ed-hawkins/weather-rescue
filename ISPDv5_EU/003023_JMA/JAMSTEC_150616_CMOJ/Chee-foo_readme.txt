Readme for Chee-foo station data
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data source 
Monthly Report of the Central Meteorological Observatory Tokyo Japan 

Datasets
  Chee-foo 3 time a day sea level pressure data 1907-1941
 
Station information
  location: 37deg 34'N 121deg 30'E (1907-1941)
  altitude: 22.8m (1907-1941)
  time: local time (+9 UTC) 1907-1912
                   (+8 UTC) 1913-1938 6
                   (+9 UTC) 1938 7-1941

  unit: +700 mmHg
  altitude adjustment  data are station pressure 
                       for sea level adjustment see CMOJ_sl_CHEE-FOO.xls
  gravity adjustment -0.5 mmHg
 

Data files
  CHEE-FOO_yyyy.xls
    yyyy: year
  CMOJ_sl_CHEE-FOO.xls


Data format
  Month day (local time) 6 14 22
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31



Data Policy
   This dataset is available for research only and not allowed to provide it to third party.
