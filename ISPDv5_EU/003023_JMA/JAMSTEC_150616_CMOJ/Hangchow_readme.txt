Readme for Hangchow station data
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data source 
Monthly Report of the Central Meteorological Observatory Tokyo Japan 

Datasets
  Hangchow 3 time a day sea level pressure data 1907-1919
 
Station information
  location: 30deg 11'N 120deg 12'E (1907-1919)
  altitude: 10.0m (1907-1919)
  time: local time (+9 UTC) 1907-1912
                   (+8 UTC) 1913-1919
  unit: +700 mmHg
  altitude adjustment  data are station pressure 
                       for sea level adjustment see CMOJ_sl_HANGCHOW.xls
  gravity adjustment -1.0 mmHg

 

Data files
  HANGCHOW_yyyy.xls
    yyyy: year
  CMOJ_sl_HANGCHOW.xls


Data format
  Month day (local time) 6 14 22
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31



Data Policy
   This dataset is available for research only and not allowed to provide it to third party.
