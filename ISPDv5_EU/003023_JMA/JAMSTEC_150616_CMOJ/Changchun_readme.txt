Readme for Changchun station data
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data source 
Monthly Report of the Central Meteorological Observatory Tokyo Japan 

Datasets
  Changchun 6 time a day sea level pressure data 1909-1932
 
Station information
  location: 43deg 48'N 125deg 29'E (1909-1920)
            43deg 55'N 125deg 18'E (1921-1932)
  altitude: 215.7m (1909-1932)
  time: local time (+9 UTC) 1909-1912
                   (+8 UTC) 1913-1932
  unit: +700 mmHg
  altitude adjustment  data are station pressure 
                       for sea level adjustment see CMOJ_sl_CHANGCHUN.xls
  gravity adjustment -0.1 mmHg
 

Data files
  CHANGCHUN_yyyy.xls
    yyyy: year
  CMOJ_sl_CHANGCHUN.xls


Data format
 1909-1912
  Month day (local time)3 7 11 15 19 23
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31


  1913-1932
  Month Day (local time) 2 6 10 14 18 22 
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31



Data Policy
   This dataset is available for research only and not allowed to provide it to third party.
