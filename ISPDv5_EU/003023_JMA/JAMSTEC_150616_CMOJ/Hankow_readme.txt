Readme for Hankow station data
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data source 
Monthly Report of the Central Meteorological Observatory Tokyo Japan 

Datasets
  Hankow 3 time a day sea level pressure data 1907-1941
 
Station information
  location: 30deg 35'N 114deg 17'E (1907-1941)
  altitude: Åc (1907-1910)
            37.0m (1911-1930)
            39.1m (1931-1941)
  time: local time (+9 UTC) 1907-1912
                   (+8 UTC) 1913-1938 6
                   (+9 UTC) 1938 7-1941
  unit: +700 mmHg
  altitude adjustment  data are station pressure 
                       for sea level adjustment see CMOJ_sl_HANKOW.xls
  gravity adjustment -1.0 mmHg

 

Data files
  HANKOW_yyyy.xls
    yyyy: year
  CMOJ_sl_HANKOW.xls


Data format
  Month day (local time) 6 14 22
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31



Data Policy
   This dataset is available for research only and not allowed to provide it to third party.
