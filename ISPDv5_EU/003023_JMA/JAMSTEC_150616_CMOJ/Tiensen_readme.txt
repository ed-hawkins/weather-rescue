Readme for Tiensen station data
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data source 
Monthly Report of the Central Meteorological Observatory Tokyo Japan 

Datasets
  Tiensen 3 time a day sea level pressure data 1907-1941
 
Station information
  location: 39deg 10'N 117eg 10'E (1907-1927)
            39deg 09'N 117eg 09'E (1928-1941)
  altitude: 5.0m (1907-1927)
            6.4m (1928-1939 11)
            4.6m (1939 12-1941)
  time: local time (+9 UTC) 1907-1912
                   (+8 UTC) 1913-1938 6
                   (+9 UTC) 1938 7-1941
  unit: +700 mmHg
  altitude adjustment  data are station pressure 
                       for sea level adjustment see CMOJ_sl_TIENSEN.xls
  gravity adjustment -0.4 mmHg

 

Data files
  TIENSEN_yyyy.xls
    yyyy: year
  CMOJ_sl_TIENSEN.xls


Data format
1907-1941
  Month day (local time) 6 14 22
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31



Data Policy
   This dataset is available for research only and not allowed to provide it to third party.
