Readme for Nanking station data
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data source 
Monthly Report of the Central Meteorological Observatory Tokyo Japan 

Datasets
  Nanking 3 time a day sea level pressure data 1907-1919
 
Station information
  location: 32deg 05'N 118deg 49'E (1907-1919)
  altitude: 32.8?m (1907-1908)
            28.5?m (1909-1910)
            16.0m (1911-1912)
            30.0m (1913-1919)
  time: local time (+9 UTC) 1907-1912
                   (+8 UTC) 1913-1919
  unit: +700 mmHg
  altitude adjustment  data are station pressure 
                       for sea level adjustment see CMOJ_sl_NANKING.xls
  gravity adjustment -0.9 mmHg

 

Data files
  NANKING_yyyy.xls
    yyyy: year
  CMOJ_sl_NANKING.xls


Data format
  Month day (local time) 6 14 22
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31



Data Policy
   This dataset is available for research only and not allowed to provide it to third party.
