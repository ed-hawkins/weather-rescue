Readme for Riojun station data
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data source 
Monthly Report of the Central Meteorological Observatory Tokyo Japan 

Datasets
  Riojun 3 time a day sea level pressure data 1907-1941
 
Station information
  location: 38deg 47'N 121deg 16'E (1907-1936)
            38deg 48'N 121deg 13'E (1937-1941)
  altitude: 17.8m (1907-1909)
            79.6m (1910-1936)
            16.1m (1937-1941)
  time: local time (+9 UTC) 1907-1912
                   (+8 UTC) 1913-1936
                   (+9 UTC) 1937-1941
  unit: +700 mmHg
  altitude adjustment  data are station pressure 
                       for sea level adjustment see CMOJ_sl_RIOJUN.xls
  gravity adjustment -0.4 mmHg

 

Data files
  RIOJUN_yyyy.xls
    yyyy: year
  CMOJ_sl_RIOJUN.xls


Data format
  Month day (local time) 6 14 22
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31




Data Policy
   This dataset is available for research only and not allowed to provide it to third party.
