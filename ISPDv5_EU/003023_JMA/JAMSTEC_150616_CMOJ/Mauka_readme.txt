Readme for Mauka station data
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data source 
Monthly Report of the Central Meteorological Observatory Tokyo Japan 

Datasets
  Mauka 3 to 6 time a day sea level pressure data 1908-1941
 
Station information
  location: 47deg 03'N 142deg 02'E (1908)
            47deg 03'N 142deg 03'E (1909-1941)
  altitude: 28.8m (1910-1929)
            29.6m (1930-1941)
  time: local time (+9 UTC) 1908-1941
  unit: +700 mmHg
  altitude adjustment  data are station pressure 
                       for sea level adjustment see CMOJ_sl_MAUKA.xls
  gravity adjustment +0.1 mmHg

 

Data files
  MAUKA_yyyy.xls
    yyyy: year
  CMOJ_sl_MAUKA.xls


Data format
1908-1909,1919-1923,1930-1941
  Month day (local time) 6 14 22
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31


1910-1918,1924-1929
  Month day (local time) 2 6 10 14 18 22
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31




Data Policy
   This dataset is available for research only and not allowed to provide it to third party.
