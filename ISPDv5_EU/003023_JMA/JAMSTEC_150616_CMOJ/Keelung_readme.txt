Readme for Keelung station data
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data source 
Monthly Report of the Central Meteorological Observatory Tokyo Japan 

Datasets
  Keelung 6 time a day sea level pressure data 1907-1916
 
Station information
  location: 25deg 09'N 121deg 45'E (1907-1916)
  altitude: 3.4m (1907-1916)
  time: local time (+8 UTC) 1907-1916
  unit: +700 mmHg
  altitude adjustment  data are station pressure 
                       for sea level adjustment see CMOJ_sl_KEELUNG.xls
  gravity adjustment -1.3 mmHg

 

Data files
  KEELUNG_yyyy.xls
    yyyy: year
  CMOJ_sl_KEELUNG.xls


Data format
  Month day (local time) 2 6 10 14 18 22
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31




Data Policy
   This dataset is available for research only and not allowed to provide it to third party.
