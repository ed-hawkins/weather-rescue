Readme for Riugampo station data
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Data source 
Monthly Report of the Central Meteorological Observatory Tokyo Japan 

Datasets
  Riugampo 3 to 6 time a day sea level pressure data 1907-1930
 
Station information
  location: 39deg 56'N 124deg 22'E (1907-1930)
  altitude: 6.4m (1907-1930)
  time: local time (+9 UTC) 1907-1930
  unit: +700 mmHg
  altitude adjustment  data are station pressure 
                       for sea level adjustment see CMOJ_sl_RIUGAMPO.xls
  gravity adjustment -0.3 mmHg

 

Data files
  RIUGAMPO_yyyy.xls
    yyyy: year
  CMOJ_sl_RIUGAMPO.xls


Data format
1907-1918,1924-1929
  Month day (local time) 2 6 10 14 18 22
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31


1919-1923,1930
  Month day (local time) 6 14 22
  1     1
  1     2
  .
  .
  12   30
  12   31



Data Policy
   This dataset is available for research only and not allowed to provide it to third party.
